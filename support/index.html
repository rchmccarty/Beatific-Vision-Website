Beatific Vision is built for perpetuity — a heritage to be inherited, not a startup to be exited. Three ways to take part in the work.

For the Audience

The Newsletter

Premieres, releases, essays from the journal, the work of the studio. Sent rarely, never marketing-speak. The first to know when Empyrean opens, when the next film ships, when the inaugural festival is announced. For those who want to follow what we make.

No spam. No sale of your address. One unsubscribe link in every message.

For the Mission

Support the Apostolate

The Catholic Film Institute is the 501(c)(3) charitable arm of Beatific Vision. Your gift forms the next generation of Catholic filmmakers — through scholarships, mentorship, our forthcoming festival, the Journal of Catholic Cinema, and eventually the Conservatory program. All gifts are tax-deductible.

Give to CFI

Donations processed securely by Qgiv. The Catholic Film Institute is a Delaware nonprofit corporation, IRS Tax-Exempt EIN 99-4577977. CFI is the charitable apostolate of Beatific Vision.

For the Studio

Become a Patron

Patron-level engagement with Beatific Vision, Inc. — equity participation in the Public Benefit Corporation, founding patronage of named productions, advisory roles. For those who would build alongside us.

Named Programs & Major Gifts

Build alongside us.
