Corporate Information

Beatific Vision, LLC is a Delaware single-member limited liability company, formed May 1, 2024. It serves as the patrimony arm of the Beatific Vision enterprise — holding intellectual property, trademarks, and serving as sole member of The Catholic Film Institute, Inc.

Beatific Vision, Inc. is a Delaware Public Benefit Corporation, reincorporated May 1, 2024. It serves as the operations arm of the Beatific Vision enterprise — focused on motion picture production, distribution, and the operation of the platforms and software bearing the BEATIFIC VISION® mark.

Both entities maintain their principal offices at 1890 1st Capitol Drive #735, Saint Charles, Missouri 63301.

Privacy

Beatific Vision collects only the minimum personal information necessary to provide its services. Specifically:

  • Newsletter subscriptions: email address, optional name. Used solely for delivering newsletter content. Never sold, never rented, never shared with third parties.
  • Donations to The Catholic Film Institute: processed by Qgiv, our payment processor. Subject to Qgiv's privacy policy.
  • Empyrean application: no telemetry, no third-party tracking, no personal data is transmitted to Beatific Vision servers. The application is fully local-first.
  • This website: minimal analytics (aggregate page-view counts only) via privacy-respecting infrastructure. No cross-site tracking. No advertising cookies.

To request deletion of any personal information you have shared with us, contact rchmccarty@beatificvision.com.
