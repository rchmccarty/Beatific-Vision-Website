To beautify the screen
is to evangelize
the age.

Beatific Vision is a Catholic film studio and integrated media enterprise founded to recover, by craft and conviction, an art form long surrendered to the indifferent. Where the Hollywood machine produces volume, we pursue depth. Where it traffics in despair, we offer wonder. Where it counterfeits the sacred, we attempt the real thing.

Our mission is not modest: to disrupt the industrial complex that has confused beauty with spectacle and meaning with sensation, and to build, in its stead, an integrated infrastructure of Catholic creative work — distribution, representation, formation, finance — under one consecrated banner. The labor will be generational. The labor of cathedrals always was.

Beneath our banner, the Catholic Film Institute trains the next generation of filmmakers. Catholica Cinematica gathers the conversation. Empyrean carries the catalog. In time, a guild. In time, an agency. In time, a distributor — every limb of an industry, made new.

The cinema is the great catechism of the modern world
— and we have surrendered the pulpit.
— Foundational Premise

Letter from the Founder

An interrupted vocation,
resumed.

Beatific Vision was conceived in 2010. In 2011, we launched the Seraphim Screenwriting Competition — the first nationwide Catholic screenwriting contest — and released our first feature-length documentary, The Most Beautiful Thing This Side of Heaven. In 2012, we hosted an inaugural national Catholic conference and began development on a dramatic feature.

And then, for a season, the work was set aside. Law called. Family called. The studio waited. The conviction never altered.

In 2024, we resumed. We re-secured the marks. We stood up the corporate architecture for perpetuity — a holding LLC for patrimony, a Public Benefit Corporation for operations, a 501(c)(3) for charitable formation. We are persuaded — fully, unreservedly persuaded — that beauty has not exhausted its evangelizing power. We mean to prove it, frame by frame.

Ryan C. McCarty
Founder · Beatific Vision · Saint Charles, MO
