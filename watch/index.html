Now Showing · Feature Documentary

The Most Beautiful Thing
This Side of Heaven

A Beatific Vision Documentary · MMXI · Restored MMXXVI · 64 Minutes

The first feature-length documentary released under the Beatific Vision banner — an account of the re-introduction of the Traditional Latin Mass into the mainstream of the Archdiocese of Kansas City, Missouri. Produced over 18 months with cooperation from priests, parishioners, and visiting laity, the film is a meditation on what is recovered when the Church recovers her own liturgical patrimony, and what was lost when she did not.

Originally released 2011. Restored from the original masters and re-released for the new platform in 2026.
