Featured · Inaugural Essay

An Unfinished Cathedral

It is sometimes said that the great age of Catholic art is behind us — that the cathedrals were built, the icons painted, the great fugues composed, and that what remains is to admire and preserve. We do not believe this. We believe the cathedral of the modern age is the cinema, and that its construction has been arrested at the foundations.

Ryan C. McCarty · 30 April · MMXXVI · 2,400 words
