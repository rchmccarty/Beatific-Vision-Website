The Seraphim Screenwriting Competition: A First Attempt

An archival note on the 2011 Seraphim Screenwriting Competition — the first nationwide Catholic screenwriting contest, advertised in Script Magazine and Creative Screenwriting, with a guaranteed greenlight on the line. Why the prize was not awarded that year, what the contest taught about the upstream pipeline, and what we are persuaded the resumed enterprise might do with it next.
